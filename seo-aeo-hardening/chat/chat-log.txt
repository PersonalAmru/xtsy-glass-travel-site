> How far is Wari Chora from Shillong?
XTSY (New Shillong) → Wari Chora canyon: about 10.5 h (~299 km) via Shillong. From Guwahati airport it’s ~10.25 h.

That’s too far for a day trip from Shillong, so plan at least 3 extra days for it, sleeping near it at a partner guesthouse in Tura or Baghmara that the XTSY desk books. Keep your Shillong nights at XTSY.

Hill roads are slow and weather can add time — the desk confirms every route.

> Where should I stay near Kaziranga?
Kaziranga National Park is ~7.5 h (~300 km) from Shillong, so you’ll sleep near it: the XTSY desk books a trusted partner lodge at Kohora, Kaziranga for you. Spend your Shillong nights with us:

1. XTSY Travellers Hostel — our own hostel on Mawdiangdiang Road, New Shillong Township (near IIM & NIFT Shillong). Dorm beds from ₹399, women-only dorm ₹599, King Double ₹2,200 a night. TAB Bistro on site, 24/7 arrival support, pets welcome (cats and birds; dogs by breed and size).

2. Experience by XTSY — India’s First Stargaze Glamp, a 270° glass suite for two at the same property: ₹5,399 stay only or ₹5,799 with breakfast for two; decoration +₹2,500.

XTSY’s own property is only in New Shillong Township; everywhere else in Northeast India, XTSY arranges stays, cabs and guides through trusted partners.
