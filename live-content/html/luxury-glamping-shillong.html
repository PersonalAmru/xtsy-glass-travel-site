    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is glamping in Shillong?

    Glamping in Shillong means luxury camping with full amenities. Experience by XTSY is India's first panoramic glass house — three glass walls offering mountain views, stargazing, and sunrise from your bed. It includes a private bathroom, queen bed, climate control, and premium breakfast.

    How much does glamping cost in Shillong?

    Experience by XTSY is ₹5,399/night for the stay only, or ₹5,799/night with breakfast for two. WiFi and all amenities are included.

    Is Experience by XTSY good for couples?

    Absolutely! Experience by XTSY is perfect for romantic getaways, anniversaries, honeymoons, and proposals. Private glass walls face natural landscapes, not buildings. Couples get sunrise views, stargazing, and complete privacy with blackout curtains.
