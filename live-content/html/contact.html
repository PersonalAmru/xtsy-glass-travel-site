    Before you message us

    WhatsApp the XTSY desk on +91 88373 17070 — it is answered day and night. You can also email booking@xtsy.one for bookings and enquiries, or admin@xtsy.one for billing, invoices and policies.

    Check-in is from 11 AM and check-out is at 10 AM. Arriving late? Message the desk on WhatsApp so we can plan your arrival.

    There is no free cancellation: the booking amount is non-refundable. You can change your dates once within 90 days of the original check-in (ask at least 48 hours before), subject to availability.

    Cats and birds are most welcome. Dogs are accepted depending on breed and size. Contact the XTSY desk before booking for any additional charges.
