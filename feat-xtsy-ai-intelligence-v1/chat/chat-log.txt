> How far is Wari Chora from Shillong?
XTSY (New Shillong) → Wari Chora canyon: about 10.5 h (~299 km) via Shillong. From Guwahati airport it’s ~10.25 h.

That’s too far for a day trip from Shillong, so plan at least 3 extra days for it, sleeping near it at a partner guesthouse in Tura or Baghmara that the XTSY desk books. Keep your Shillong nights at XTSY.

Hill roads are slow and weather can add time — the desk confirms every route.
